# Harlo pricing research

Current comparable pricing as of May 2, 2026:

| Product | Entry paid | Mid plan | Scale plan | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Chatbase | $32/mo annual, $40/mo monthly | $120/mo annual, $150/mo monthly | $400/mo annual, $500/mo monthly | 500, 4,000, and 15,000 monthly message credits on paid tiers. |
| ChatNode | $35/mo | $89/mo | $377/mo | 1,250, 12,500, and 50,000 monthly credits; higher tiers add auto-retrain, domains, branding removal, and priority support. |
| SiteGPT | $39/mo | $79/mo | $259/mo | 4,000, 10,000, and 40,000 monthly messages; scales mostly by pages, chatbots, refresh cadence, and team seats. |
| Botsonic | $19/mo monthly | $49/mo monthly | $299/mo monthly | 1,000, 3,000, and 12,000 monthly messages; advanced tier adds workflows, auto-sync, API access, and onboarding. |
| Intercom Fin | $29+/seat plus usage | $85+/seat plus usage | $132+/seat plus usage | Fin prices around $0.99 per resolved outcome, so it sits above SMB chatbot builders. |

Recommended Harlo public plans:

| Plan | Price | Included usage | Positioning |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Starter | $39/mo | 1 chatbot, 1,000 AI conversations, 100 knowledge pages | Matches the market entry band without racing to the bottom. |
| Growth | $89/mo | 2 chatbots, 5,000 AI conversations, 1,000 knowledge pages | Main SMB plan; competitive with ChatNode Standard and SiteGPT Growth. |
| Pro | $249/mo | 5 chatbots, 20,000 AI conversations, 10,000 knowledge pages | Below Chatbase Pro and ChatNode Scale while leaving margin for support-heavy customers. |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom usage, roles, onboarding, SLA, DPA, integrations | Keeps procurement, compliance, and custom workflow deals out of self-serve pricing. |

Suggested packaging rules:

- Offer annual billing at 2 months free.
- Charge overages or top-ups at $20 per 1,000 AI conversations.
- Keep custom domain, API access, priority support, and white-label controls out of Starter.
- Make Growth the default recommended plan.
